# No slop

Page content belongs to the app. Write only text the app needs, about what the app actually does. The skill supplies the frame and components, never the words, numbers or features.

Every visible string is one of: a label, a value, an instruction needed to act, a consequence, or an error with its recovery. Anything else is slop. Cut:

- Subtitles, eyebrow labels and taglines nobody asked for. A title stands alone.
- Disclaimers, banners and intro paragraphs.
- Explanatory sentences describing what a control does. Put a real explanation behind a "?" if one is needed.
- Claim-heavy adjectives ("practical", "focused", "sharp", "tested") and self-promotion.
- A page title repeated when the navigation already shows the page.
- Filler Dashboards, summary cards and invented metrics.
- Fake features, mock data and unmarked stand-in content. A feature is complete or absent; stand-in content during development is a Stub.

How the rest reads:

- Plain words at about an eighth-grade level. Explain an unavoidable term in parentheses.
- Sentence case for UI labels. Short sentences; no mid-sentence dashes; no emojis; no first person.
- Copy matches how the product actually works right now.
